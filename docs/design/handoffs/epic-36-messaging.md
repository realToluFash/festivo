## What this area is

One machine: a message has an audience, a set of channels, a template and a trigger. An
invitation is a message triggered by publishing; a reminder by a clock; an announcement by
a person.

## Candidate artboards

- **Messaging** — composing, and the delivery record

## Layouts, and why

**All four.** Composed on a computer, read and answered from the apps, received anywhere.

## What these screens must honour

- The audience is built from conditions, with **a live count** as it is built.
- **Push rides along with another channel** and is never a channel on its own.
- A template shows merge fields as **tokens**, not raw braces, and the same message exists
  in four languages.
- A preview is **what the person actually receives**, per channel — email, SMS, WhatsApp,
  an in-app item and a web page are five genuinely different renderings.
- Before sending: reach, cost, and **who cannot be reached at all**.

## Traps specific to this epic

- **The delivery record matters more than the composer.** "My guests never got it" is the
  thing we expect to be asked about most, and **why a channel was skipped** is the answer.
  A design that draws the composer beautifully and the log as a table is the wrong way
  round.
- Sending to four hundred people is irreversible and costs money. The last step before it
  goes carries real weight.
- A reply is a conversation, not a row in a log.
