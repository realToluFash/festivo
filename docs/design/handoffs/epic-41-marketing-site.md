## What this area is

For somebody who has never heard of Soirely, and for a guest who arrives from a link in an
email. Neither has an account, and neither will ever install anything.

## Candidate artboards

- **Public Pages** — the marketing and sign-up pages
- **Guest RSVP** — the guest's own pages

## Layouts, and why

**Desktop and phone browser, both fully.** This is the one surface where the phone browser
is the primary case rather than the exception.

## What these screens must honour

- **What this is, in one screen**, in the reader's language.
- **What it costs, before being asked for anything.**
- The guest path is **the simplest thing in the product**: one task at a time, nothing to
  learn, and no sense of being at the edge of a large system.
- A guest may be elderly and mildly irritated at being asked for anything. The guest body
  size is larger than the app's on purpose.

## Traps specific to this epic

- A guest list of four hundred is **four hundred first impressions**, and the organiser's
  reputation is in it. This is the part of the product most people ever see.
- Four locales on a public page, with a language picker, because somebody who cannot read
  the page cannot sign up.
- A guest returning to change their mind is a normal flow, not an edge case.
